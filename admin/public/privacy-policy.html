BornoVerse Privacy Policy

A Universe Worth Listening To.

Effective date: 5 November 2026

This policy explains what information the BornoVerse mobile app (package com.bornoverse.app) and its services collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using BornoVerse you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, username, email address and a secure hash of your password (we never store the password itself). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account identifier.
  • Profile and preferences: profile picture, app language, favourite genres and channels, followed channels and notification settings.
  • Listening activity: library items, playlists, playback position and listening time. Listening time is used to calculate publisher earnings.
  • Content you upload: documents, images, cover pictures, titles and descriptions you submit to create audiobooks. Cover pictures are chosen from your photo library only when you pick one; the app does not use your camera or microphone.
  • Payment records: plan, amount, status and transaction reference for Premium purchases and publisher fees. Card or mobile-wallet details are entered on the SSLCommerz payment page and are not stored by BornoVerse.
  • Publisher information: if you apply to become a publisher, your legal name and the verification document you upload, and for withdrawals the payout method, account name, account number, bank name and branch.
  • Reports and appeals: content reports, moderation decisions and appeals you submit.
  • Device information: a push-notification token for your device, used only to send you notifications.

2. How we use information

  • Create and secure your account, including sign-in, email verification and password reset.
  • Convert your uploaded documents into audiobooks and play them back.
  • Save your library and listening progress and recommend audiobooks.
  • Process Premium subscriptions, publisher fees, earnings and withdrawals.
  • Send notifications and service emails you need, such as receipts and reminders.
  • Moderate content, prevent abuse and keep an administrative audit log.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show third-party advertising.

3. Who we share it with

We share only what each service needs to do its job:

  • Google (Gemini API): text and page images from documents you upload, to read the text and generate speech.
  • Google Sign-In: to verify your identity when you choose to sign in with Google.
  • SSLCommerz: to process payments.
  • Email provider: your email address, to deliver account and service emails.
  • Expo push service: your device push token, to deliver notifications.
  • Hosting, database and file-storage providers (such as Render, Vercel and Supabase), which store and serve the app's data on our behalf.
  • Other users: audiobooks you publish, your channel name, channel picture and description are public inside the app.
  • Authorities: when required by law.

4. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed, sign-in tokens are kept in your device's secure storage, and administrative access is restricted to authorised staff. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

5. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, it is removed immediately (see below). Verification and reset codes expire automatically.

6. Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account at any time from inside the app:

  • Open Profile and choose Delete account.
  • Type DELETE to confirm.

This immediately removes your account, profile, library, listening history, follows, playlists, notifications, push tokens, payment and payout records, and the content you uploaded. The admin audit log keeps a record of past actions, but it is no longer linked to you. SSLCommerz keeps its own payment records under its own policy. Full details and an email option are on the account deletion page.

7. Your choices

  • Change your name, picture, language and preferences in Profile.
  • Turn notifications off in the app or in your device settings.
  • Revoke photo-library access in your device settings at any time.

8. Children

BornoVerse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and tell you in the app. Continuing to use BornoVerse after a change means you accept the updated policy.
