Delete your BornoVerse account

BornoVerse app by Smart Tutors

You can permanently delete your BornoVerse account and its data at any time. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Steps to delete your account

  1. Open the BornoVerse app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then confirm the deletion.

Can't open the app? Email salehsadid16@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject “BornoVerse account deletion”. We will delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Data that is deleted

  • Your account: name, username, email address, password hash and profile picture.
  • Your library, playlists, listening progress and listening history.
  • Followed channels, favourite genres, preferences, notifications and device push tokens.
  • Audiobooks, channels, covers and documents you uploaded, including the generated audio.
  • Payment and subscription records, publisher verification details, earnings and payout details.
  • Reports and appeals you submitted.

Data that is kept

  • Administrative audit log: records of actions taken on the platform are kept so we can keep the service secure, but they are no longer linked to your account or identity.
  • Payment provider records: SSLCommerz keeps its own records of any payment you made, under its own policy and as required by law.

There is no other retention period: everything else is removed as soon as you confirm the deletion.
